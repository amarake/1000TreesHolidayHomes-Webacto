About the Project

Build Your Dream Holiday Home

1000 Trees Holiday Homes offers 74 NA plots in Dapoli, Ratnagiri district, ready for you to buy and build on. These Dapoli NA plots range from 2,000 to 5,600 sq ft, and every plot has water, MSEB power and four fruit trees already in place. If you are looking for plots in Dapoli or NA plots in the Konkan with clear paperwork, this project brings the NA order, individual 7/12 and registered sale deed together with ready infrastructure. You can also build a bungalow in Dapoli on your plot — and a bank loan covers the plot and the bungalow together.
